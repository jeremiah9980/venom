How this page works. Two background workflows keep this portal current. The NCS tracker pulls each team's tournament list hourly and checks the live event schedule every 10 minutes — the moment NCS releases pool-play game times, they show up inside the matching weekend block. A separate workflow pulls the GameChanger calendar feed every 30 minutes for practices, games, and team events. If something looks missing, GameChanger and playncs.com are the sources of truth — fix it there and it appears here on the next sync. See GC_INTEGRATION.md for setup details.
